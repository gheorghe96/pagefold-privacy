Pagefold Support

Your trips, in a real notebook.

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Send us a message and include your device model and iOS or Android version. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I add photos and videos to a page?

Open an album, tap the pencil to edit the page, then tap Photos. You can pick many photos and videos at once.

How do I move, resize or rotate something?

In the editor, drag an item to move it, pinch to resize and twist with two fingers to rotate. You can also drag the round handle on its corner with one finger. Tap Frame to change its frame, or Caption to write under a photo.

How do I watch a video with sound?

Videos play silently on the page. Tap a video to watch it full screen with sound. You can stop videos from playing on pages in Settings → Play videos on pages.

My albums aren't showing on my other device

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, iCloud is turned on, and Settings → Sync with iCloud is on in Pagefold. Open the app on both devices; a small "Syncing" bar appears while changes transfer. Large videos can take a while on slow connections.

Android: how does sync work?

On Android, Pagefold syncs with Google Drive instead of iCloud. Open Settings → Sync with Google Drive and sign in with your Google account. Your albums are stored in a private app folder in your own Drive that only Pagefold can access. Sign in with the same Google account on your other Android devices to see them there.

Will I lose my albums if I delete the app?

With sync on (iCloud on iPhone and iPad, Google Drive on Android), reinstalling Pagefold restores your albums. With sync off, deleting the app deletes them.
