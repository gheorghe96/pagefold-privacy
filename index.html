Pagefold Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Short version: Pagefold has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your albums, photos, videos and notes stay on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud storage. We can't see any of it.

Who we are

Pagefold is an iOS and iPadOS app published by Gheorghe Curcă ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this page.

Your albums, photos and videos

Albums, pages, text, and the photos and videos you add are stored inside the app on your device. Pagefold uses Apple's photo picker, so the app only receives the photos and videos you choose; it never reads the rest of your library. Deleting the app deletes the data stored on that device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and keep Settings → Sync with iCloud turned on, Pagefold stores a copy of your albums, photos and videos in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices and can be restored after reinstalling. This data is protected by your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. It is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn sync off in the app, and you can delete the stored data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Pagefold.

Saving and sharing

When you share a page as an image, iOS shows its share sheet; nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a destination. If you choose "Save Image", iOS asks for permission to add the image to your Photos library.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Children

Pagefold doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is under your control on your device and in your iCloud account. Where EU/UK law applies, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
