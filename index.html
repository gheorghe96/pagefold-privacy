Pagefold Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Short version: Pagefold has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your albums, photos, videos and notes stay on your device and, with Pagefold Pro sync, in your own private iCloud storage (iPhone and iPad) or a private app folder in your own Google Drive (Android). We can't see any of it. If you buy Pagefold Pro, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play and recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID, so your purchase can be confirmed and restored.

Who we are

Pagefold is an app for iOS, iPadOS and Android published by Gheorghe Curcă ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this page.

Your albums, photos and videos

Albums, pages, text, and the photos and videos you add are stored inside the app on your device. Pagefold uses the system photo picker (Apple's photo picker on iOS, the Android photo picker on Android), so the app only receives the photos and videos you choose; it never reads the rest of your library. Deleting the app deletes the data stored on that device.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is part of Pagefold Pro. If you have Pro, are signed in to iCloud and keep Settings → Sync with iCloud turned on, Pagefold stores a copy of your albums, photos and videos in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices and can be restored after reinstalling. This data is protected by your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. It is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn sync off in the app, and you can delete the stored data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Pagefold.

Google Drive sync (Android)

Google Drive sync is part of Pagefold Pro. On Android, if you have Pro and turn on Settings → Sync with Google Drive, Pagefold asks Google for permission to use one Google Drive scope only: drive.appdata. This lets Pagefold create and read files in a hidden application data folder in your own Google Drive that only Pagefold can access. Pagefold cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and it does not request your name, email address, contacts or any other Google account data.

Pagefold uses this folder only to store a copy of your albums, pages, notes, photos and videos, so they appear on your other Android devices signed in with the same Google account and can be restored after reinstalling. The files go directly between your device and Google; they never pass through our servers (we have none), we never have access to them, and they are not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone. This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

You can turn sync off in the app at any time. To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps, find Pagefold and choose Delete hidden app data. You can remove Pagefold's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Pagefold's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Pagefold Pro purchases

Your first album is free. Pagefold Pro (monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) adds unlimited albums and sync. Payment is made through Apple's App Store or Google Play under their terms; we never see your name, email address, card or other payment details.

To confirm purchases and restore them on your other devices or after reinstalling, Pagefold uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which processes this data on our behalf:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name, email or Apple / Google account details);
  • purchase information: which product, price and currency, purchase and renewal dates, and the store's transaction records and identifiers;
  • basic technical data needed to make the request: app version, operating system version, device model, country or region, language and IP address.

This data is used only to unlock Pagefold Pro, restore purchases, handle renewals and refunds, and give us aggregate sales reports. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. It is handled under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and stored on RevenueCat's servers, which may be outside your country. Your albums, photos, videos and notes are never sent to RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings. To ask us to delete your purchase record, contact us (see below) and include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.

Saving and sharing

When you share a page as an image, the system share sheet opens; nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a destination. On iOS, if you choose "Save Image", iOS asks for permission to add the image to your Photos library.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics. The only third-party SDK is RevenueCat, used solely to process in-app purchases.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Children

Pagefold doesn't ask for or knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Purchases, if any, are recorded only under an anonymous ID as described above.

Your rights

Your albums and media are under your control on your device and in your iCloud account or Google Drive; we hold none of them. The only data processed for us is the anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat, which we can delete on request. Where EU/UK law applies, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
